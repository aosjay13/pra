The League

Built for drivers who care about the data.

Prodigy Racing Association isn't another casual lobby. We're a community of sim racers who value clean racing, tight competition, and detailed record-keeping — with an independent steward panel reviewing incidents after every race.

We've moved beyond messy spreadsheets. The league runs on the custom-built Phoenix Racing League Manager: live standings, season schedules, career stats, track records and driver skill ratings, all in one place.
